Does a Small Model Have a Global Workspace?

Summer 2026 · Mechanistic interpretability · Negative results

I pre-registered Anthropic’s J-space ablation on Qwen3-4B. The lens directions are causally real (deleting them beats norm-matched random damage cleanly), but the workspace/autopilot separation the paper’s chain-of-thought result rests on never shows up: ordinary next-token prediction breaks alongside reasoning. The split may be something models grow into with scale.
